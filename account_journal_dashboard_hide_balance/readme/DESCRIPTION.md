This module hides the amounts shown on the bank, cash and credit card
journals of the accounting dashboard (balance, last statement, outstanding
payments and miscellaneous operations), the same way banking apps do.

The amounts are displayed as `*****` and an eye icon appears in the top
bar, next to the messages and activities icons. One click on the eye
reveals the amounts of every journal, and another click hides them
again. They are hidden again when the page is reloaded.

This is a display feature: it keeps balances away from people looking
at the screen, it does not restrict access to them. Use access rights to
control who can see the journals.
