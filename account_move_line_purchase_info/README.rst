This module shows the purchase order and the purchase order line on the
journal items, so accounting entries can be traced back to the purchase
they come from.

In Odoo 19 the stock valuation has changed and no longer goes through
the stock interim accounts, and Odoo already links the vendor bill lines
to their purchase order line. Because of that this module has been
largely simplified in this version:

What is kept:

- The purchase order and purchase order line information on the journal
  items, together with the dedicated access group that controls who can
  see it.
- The possibility to group and filter the journal items by purchase
  order and by purchase order line.

What is dropped:

- The purchase information on the journal items generated by the stock
  valuation and by the price differences, since those entries are no
  longer created by Odoo.
- The link to the journal entries from the purchase order.

Usage
=====

Give the users that need the purchase information the *Purchase info in
Journal Items* access group.

Those users will then see the purchase order and the purchase order line
on the journal items and on the journal entries, and they will be able
to group and filter the journal items by them.
