1. Go to *Settings > Users* and enable *Accounting Entry Review / Entry Reviewer* for
   designated reviewers.
2. Optionally, go to *Accounting > Configuration > Settings > Audit Trail* and enable
   *Restrict Reset to Draft to Reviewers* per company to block non-reviewers from
   resetting posted entries and payments to draft.
3. Reviewers open any posted entry, invoice, bill, or payment and either click
   *Mark Reviewed* (or run the batch list action) or click *Return for Revision* to
   reset the entry to draft with optional notes sent directly to the creator.
4. Use the *Reviewed*, *Not Reviewed*, *Returned for Revision*, *Documentary*, and
   *Non-Documentary* search filters across all accounting views.
