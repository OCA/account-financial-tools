This module adds an independent entry review step on posted journal
entries, invoices, vendor bills, and payments:

- Dedicated *Entry Reviewer* security group outside the Accounting
  category (implies Accounting Manager without being granted to regular
  accountants).
- Optional per-company setting to restrict *Reset to Draft* on posted
  moves and payments to reviewers.
- One-click *Mark Reviewed* action (single and list batch) with no
  notification noise.
- Reset-to-draft wizard with optional notes and direct chatter
  notification to the entry creator.
- Optional *Document Type* classification (``Documentary`` /
  ``Non-Documentary``) editable on draft and posted entries.
- Persistent filters and grouping across moves, invoices, journal items,
  and payments.

Usage
=====

1. Go to *Settings > Users* and enable *Accounting Entry Review / Entry
   Reviewer* for designated reviewers.
2. Optionally, go to *Accounting > Configuration > Settings > Audit
   Trail* and enable *Restrict Reset to Draft to Reviewers* per company
   to block non-reviewers from resetting posted entries and payments to
   draft.
3. Reviewers open any posted entry, invoice, bill, or payment and either
   click *Mark Reviewed* (or run the batch list action) or click *Return
   for Revision* to reset the entry to draft with optional notes sent
   directly to the creator.
4. Use the *Reviewed*, *Not Reviewed*, *Returned for Revision*,
   *Documentary*, and *Non-Documentary* search filters across all
   accounting views.
