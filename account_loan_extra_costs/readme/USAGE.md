On a loan, open the *Extra Costs* tab and add one line per cost.
Choose the payment type (Upfront or Periodic) and a calculation method
(Fixed amount, Rate on initial capital, Rate on remaining capital per
period, or Rate on remaining capital at anniversary).

Upfront costs are added to the opening entry. Periodic costs appear on
each installment.

Extra cost amounts can be edited manually on each installment as long
as the entry has not been generated.
