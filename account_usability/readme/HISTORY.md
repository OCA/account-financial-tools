## 20.0.1.0.0 (2026-09-29)

> - \[BREAKING\] The `account.group` model has been removed from Odoo 20.0
>   and replaced by the account parent hierarchy (`account.account.parent_id`).
>   Therefore the module no longer adds an **Account Groups** menu and the
>   related `account_ids` field on account groups.
