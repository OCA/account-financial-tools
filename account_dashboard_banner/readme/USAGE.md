Enjoy the accounting dashboard with the banner at the top:

![Accounting dashboard with banner](../static/description/account_dashboard_banner.png)

When several companies are selected in the company switcher, each cell
shows the total of the selected companies and, under it, the value of each
company, in its own currency. The total is in the currency of the current
company: the amounts of the companies in other currencies are converted at
today's rate. Lock dates are set per company: a lock date cell shows the oldest lock
date of the selected companies, up to which all of them are locked.
